<Callout type="info">OAuth-capable — `TUMBLR_CLIENT_ID` / `TUMBLR_CLIENT_SECRET`.</Callout>

## Register the app (deployment owner, one time)

<Steps>
<Step>Go to [tumblr.com/oauth/apps](https://www.tumblr.com/oauth/apps) → **Register application**.</Step>
<Step>Set the redirect URL — see [Publish overview](/docs/publish/overview#two-ways-to-connect-a-platform).</Step>
<Step>Copy the **OAuth Consumer Key** and **Secret Key** into `TUMBLR_CLIENT_ID` / `TUMBLR_CLIENT_SECRET`.</Step>
</Steps>

## Or connect manually

Paste an **Access Token** plus the blog identifier (e.g.
`yourblog.tumblr.com`) on **Publish → Connections → Tumblr**.
