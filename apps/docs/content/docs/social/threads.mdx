title: Threads

---

<Callout type="info">
OAuth-capable — `THREADS_CLIENT_ID` / `THREADS_CLIENT_SECRET`. Its own
credential pair, even though it lives in the same Meta App dashboard used
for [Facebook Pages](/docs/social/facebook).
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>In your Meta App dashboard: add the **Threads API** product.</Step>
<Step>Add the redirect URL — see [Social overview](/docs/social/overview#two-ways-to-connect-a-platform). Scopes `threads_basic`, `threads_content_publish` are requested automatically.</Step>
<Step>Copy the **Threads App ID** / **Threads App Secret** into `THREADS_CLIENT_ID` / `THREADS_CLIENT_SECRET`.</Step>
</Steps>

## Or connect manually

Paste an **Access Token** on **Social → Connections → Threads**.

Character limit: **500**.
